# Conflict Check

Conflict Check accepts a name, document identifier, company and related names. It performs firm-scoped, case-insensitive matching across active/archived clients, contacts, representatives, Matter parties (including opposing parties), archived Matters and Matter titles.

Results identify the matched entity, relationship, related Matter/status and responsible professional when known. The response uses **Possible conflict**. It does not make a legal determination, block intake automatically or replace professional investigation.

Namesakes, incomplete spelling, corporate groups, former names and missing historical data produce false positives or negatives. Users must review results, expand related names and record their professional decision outside the matching algorithm. Future search improvements may normalize accents and aliases without changing that boundary.
